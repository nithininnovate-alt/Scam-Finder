---
name: PRD interpretation
description: Rationale for resolving the attached phased product requirements and conflicting retention clauses.
---

The attached PRD is a multi-phase roadmap, not evidence that its accuracy, compliance, and forensic-certification targets have been achieved.

**Why:** Its go-live checklist requires labeled benchmarks, external investigation review, and privacy sign-off that are not supplied with the requirements.

**How to apply:** Describe actual capabilities and limitations; do not convert roadmap targets into marketing claims.

The attached requirements conflict on routine retention: privacy requirements specify 90 days, while the SOP specifies 12 months. Favor privacy-minimizing routine retention unless the user resolves the conflict differently.

**Why:** Keeping sensitive job letters longer without clarification would silently weaken the stricter privacy requirement. Escalated-case retention remains a separate explicit exception.

**How to apply:** Flag this conflict during future compliance or analyst-workflow work rather than assuming the longer SOP duration takes precedence.
